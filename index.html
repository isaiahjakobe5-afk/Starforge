STARFORGE

You are a young star. Everything you shed becomes stardust. Catch it, spend it, and see how far it goes.

  • Your star sheds stardust on its own. The dish below catches it.
  • Spend it on upgrades. Tap Upgrades. Everything you buy brings in more.
  • Your star ages, and you choose when it dies. Let it die young and it leaves a white dwarf. Wait, and it leaves a neutron star. Wait longer still, and a black hole.
Sound is on. You can turn it off in Settings.
